# Spirit in the Open

A training manual for bringing spiritual practice into public spaces and oppressed communities. From the writing of Jeff Kisling.

The manual is for young adults: Friends, people of other faiths, and people of no faith. It uses the structure of the Quaker Social Change Ministry model, and the stories of North Meadow Circle of Friends and the Kheprw Institute.
